# Leila Razavi — Website Principles

- The website is for people first; SEO supports the experience and must not make the UI harder to understand.
- Each page should have a distinct purpose and substantial content of its own. Do not repeat page content across Home and inner pages.
- Home introduces the person and explains the main areas in concise descriptive copy, then links to the relevant full pages. It does not reproduce article cards, publication entries, CV details, or other inner-page content.
- Publications contains the complete verified publication set. Home may describe the research themes and link to Publications, but should not duplicate publication records.
- Media contains the complete verified media/podcast content. Home explains what the media work is and links to it rather than copying episode records.
- About explains the professional story and identity in depth; Home should not repeat it.
- Keep navigation and page structure simple. Prefer one clear view and obvious next steps over excessive sections, cards, labels, or visual categorization.
- Typography must be readable, coherent between Persian and Latin text, and hierarchical without feeling decorative or academic for its own sake.
- Use real verified data. Never invent credentials, publications, numbers, quotations, or professional claims.
- Preserve canonical URLs, bilingual hreflang, structured data, sitemap, and the stable Person entity while improving the front end.
- Before release, verify build, routes, SEO output, responsive behavior, accessibility, and production rendering.
